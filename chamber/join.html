  • All basic networking opportunities

  • Priority access to training opportunities
  • Eligibility for homepage business spotlights

Annual membership: COP 700,000

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Premium homepage spotlight opportunities
  • Exclusive networking events
  • Featured business promotion
  • Priority registration and event discounts
